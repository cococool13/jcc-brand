## Key rules

- Body text: gray-9 `#6D6E71` or ink/navy on white and light washes. Cool Gray 7 print `#A7A9AC` fails on white: print only, never web text.
- Orange `#F68C1F` is never text on light backgrounds. On light, use `#9A560C` text on the `#FEF3E5` chip. Orange text/accents are fine on navy.
- Orange buttons use **navy** text (not white). White on orange fails AA.
- Light blue `#27AAE1` as text: only on navy/blue-ink (e.g. the word "Creative." in the tagline). On white it is decorative only.
- White text on brand blue `#1B75BC` passes AA (primary button). White on sky `#27AAE1` fails: use sky only as a fill or gradient end behind large white text with the darker blue end under the text.
- Focus states: 3px ring `rgba(27,117,188,.25)` plus a visible border change; never rely on color alone.
- Logos need descriptive alt text: "Jacobs, Coolidge & Company".
